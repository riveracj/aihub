# AI Hub Lite Mode — Messenger-Style UX Architecture

## Product Direction

The AI Hub should behave similarly to modern messaging platforms like:

* Messenger
* Telegram
* Discord
* WhatsApp

BUT optimized for:

* AI discovery
* AI conversations
* AI personas
* AI communities

The goal is:
Users should instantly understand the app without learning a new system.

---

# Core UX Philosophy

## Make AI Hubs Feel Like People

Instead of:

```text
Browsing AI tools
```

Users should feel:

```text
Talking to intelligent personalities
```

This increases:

* engagement
* retention
* emotional connection
* session duration

---

# Recommended App Structure

# Main Navigation

```text
Chats | Discover | Profile
```

Very minimal.

Reason:
Messenger-style apps succeed because:

* navigation is simple
* conversations are central
* discovery is lightweight
